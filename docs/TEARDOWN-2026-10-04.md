## 🎯 The Rebuild Question

**If we built it today, knowing what got used, it would be a phone-first face (and hands) puppet.** Open a link, allow the camera, and within a minute your face drives a stylized puppet you can record with voice, replay and share. Its core would be one tracker that runs once per camera frame, gives the face priority over the hands under load, and keeps speech-band mouth motion intact. Playback would use one typed take shape from live to buffer to file. The hand demos and the Blender export would stay a frozen "lab" path on the same `TrackedFrame`. It would measure visits, takes started and takes saved before adding features. It would run offline.

**Diff vs. reality:**
- The tracker detected every rAF, even when the camera had no new frame. Under load it halved the face, not the hands.
- The face filter is one global One Euro filter in absolute image coordinates, tuned on desktop. Lips get the same heavy filtering as the head.
- The mouth gate looked only at the lip gap measured on smoothed landmarks. The unsmoothed `jawOpen` blendshape was read but did not gate anything.
- The docs (NORTH_STAR, README, PLANNING) describe the September product.
- Nothing measures use.

## 🔍 Findings (ranked)

### 🔴 Actively costing

1. **The mouth gate hides speech.** ⚙️ *Verified.*
   - `mouthState.ts:12-13` opens the mouth only when the inner-lip gap is above 0.08 of mouth width. It reads that gap from *smoothed* landmarks.
   - `teethGap` (`puppetState.ts:122`) and `boostJaw` (`:193`) return nothing while the mouth is "closed". So Jaw Boost could not help small openings, even though its tooltip (`FaceDemo.tsx:665`) promised it would.
   - On Grayson's real 09-22 take, the old gate held the mouth shut on 26 of 275 frames where `jawOpen > 0.06`. That take is from a 59 fps desktop, which is the easy case.

2. **The face filter attenuates lip motion.** ⚙️ *Sim verified by hand.*
   - Our One Euro filter (`oneEuro.ts:18`, 0.5 Hz / beta 40 / dCutoff 1, absolute coordinates) passes only **0.34** of a 4 Hz, 5 mm lip motion at 30 fps, and **0.27** at 15 fps.
   - *Lens-sourced, read from the tasks-vision 0.10.9 C++ graph:* MediaPipe already smooths face landmarks in VIDEO mode with `numFaces: 1`. Modelled together, the gain falls to about 0.10–0.16. MediaPipe computes blendshapes from the *unsmoothed* landmarks, which is why `jawOpen` is the cleaner signal.

3. **Under load, the face got the half rate.** ⚙️ *Verified.*
   - `useTracker.ts:531-532` with `facePolicy.ts` ran the face on alternate ticks whenever the loop averaged under 30 fps. Hand detection (with `numHands: 2`, so the palm detector runs every tick) stayed at full rate.
   - That matches the 10-03 report that the face gets worse with a hand in view.

4. **Duplicate detection of the same frame.** ⚙️ *Verified.*
   - `useTracker.ts:527` checked only `videoWidth > 0`. At 60 Hz rAF on a 30 fps camera, every frame was detected twice. That wasted inference, inflated `loop fps`, and made the filter behave differently on desktop than on a phone.
   - Anything tuned on desktop therefore under-delivered on the phone.

5. **Docs are out of date and goal changes skip the ADR process.** 🧭 🏛️ *Verified.*
   - Only ADR-0001 exists.
   - NORTH_STAR.md:21 cites the deleted `useFaceTracker.ts`. :44 says "no importer" (one exists since 09-16). :59 lists "Not … networked" as a non-goal (`server/` ships). :61 says desktop is primary (the default route is the phone-first Face Puppet).
   - PLANNING.md was last touched 09-22.

6. **Nothing measures use.** 📈 *Lens-sourced.* No analytics, no Caddy log review, uploads off, repo traffic 0 views in 14 days. The north-star test measures a clone-the-repo path that no one is shown to use.

## 🧭 North Star (draft for ADR-0002, every inference ⚠️ CONFIRM)

> Puppeteer Lab is a **phone-first** ⚠️ CONFIRM face-and-hands puppet. Open a link, allow the camera, and within a minute your face drives a stylized puppet you can record with voice, replay and share as a link. The audience is anyone with the URL ⚠️ CONFIRM.
>

## 🔧 Done in the same session (not part of the reviewed state)

- **Mouth gate:** opens when the lip ratio is above 0.08 **or** raw `jawOpen` is above 0.06. It closes only when both are shut (lip ratio under 0.05 and jaw under 0.03).
  - Calibrated on Grayson's 09-22 take. Closed `jawOpen` reads 0.005–0.02. Of 236 resting frames, 0 open. Speaking frames held shut went from 26/275 to 0/275.
  - Takes without blendshapes fall back to the lip ratio alone.
- **Lip filter:** the One Euro `fast` points (lips) use 6x `minCutoff` (3 Hz at the default slider) and dCutoff 2. This raises 4 Hz gain from 0.34 to 0.68 at 30 fps, and from 0.27 to 0.59 at 15 fps. The head filter is unchanged. The filter's inner loop also no longer allocates two arrays per landmark.
- **Cost policy:** under load the **hands** run on alternate ticks and the face runs every tick. "Under load" is now measured as model cost (`handMs + faceMs` over 33 ms on, under 22 ms off), not as tick interval: with the dedupe, ticks follow camera frames, so a 30 fps camera always reads 33 ms apart and the old rule would have latched on. Only Face Puppet runs both models, so the other demos are unaffected. Seen in a browser: real GPU, 12 ms of cost, no ALT; software GPU, 180 ms of cost, ALT on.
- **One detection per camera frame:** `video.currentTime` dedupe, so `loop fps` is now honest. If a stream's `currentTime` stops advancing for 500 ms, the dedupe switches off for that stream, so tracking can never stall. This was seen in new-headless Chromium, whose fake camera reports `currentTime` 0 forever.
- **Boost defaults at max** (Brow, Jaw, Blink = 100%), as Grayson asked, in Face Puppet and the share viewer.
- **SPEAKING readout** uses the same `jawOpen` gate.
- **DEBUG overlay:** shows `hand N fps ALT` instead of `face … ALT`.
- **Gates:**
  - tsc clean.
  - 555 tests. The known `recordingSchema` timing flake passes alone, 39/39.
  - facedemo-check 27/27 (rest 9.11%, drag 28.29%).
  - orbit-check 11/11.
  - phone-check 97/97 on 4 of 6 runs. The 2 misses were one check each: once while another browser run was competing for the machine, and once in the mic check "after Turn on: one camera track, one microphone track" (audio 0). The mic path is async and untouched by this change. Flagged as intermittent.
- **Blink at 100%** on the 09-22 take: a lid is snapped shut on 125 of 768 frames, against 99 at 50%. Raw blink is above 0.5 on 93 frames. A few more squints read as closed.
- **Jaw at 100%:** teeth are fully apart at `jaw` of about 0.05. Speech now goes from teeth together to fully apart almost at once. The slider brings it back.
- **Not proven by any gate:** the lip filter and the dedupe. Playback uses already-filtered landmarks, and no harness here has a real face on camera. These are proven only on Grayson's phone.
- **Proof:** `.proof/2026-10-04-mouth/mouth-before-after.png` (live vs local, same real take, same frames).
