## 📌 Where we stopped
Phone performance is the open issue. Grayson tried the overlay build on his phone and said performance "doesn't seem much better" (nothing was changed to fix it; the overlay only measures; he did not pass on the overlay numbers, so the `facePolicy` alternation hypothesis in the first bullet is still unconfirmed). He then asked to make sure capture runs at the highest frame rate the phone offers (60 fps where available) for finer animation: **not started**. Facts for it: `useTracker.getStream` asks `width: ideal 640, height: ideal 480` and no `frameRate`, so the browser default applies (usually 30); a take records one frame per tracker tick (measured fps is stored in schema v3), so a faster camera only raises capture precision if the tracker loop keeps up, and the loop is the suspected bottleneck. Not verified: a real iPhone for touch Orbit / Clean up near the 100 s cap, and depth quality on a real take with face and hands.
