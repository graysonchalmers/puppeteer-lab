### 2026-09-16 (Task 2) - Worker serialization + chunked fallback (TDD-002 P2, item 6)
- Continuing from Task 1 (`9652985`), plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-16-item6-schema-v3-blender-importer.md`. New `components/shared/serialize.worker.ts` imports Task 1's `buildEnvelope`/`buildKinematics`/`serializeV3` unchanged and posts back a `Blob`. `hooks/useRecorder.ts`'s `exportData` is now `async`: tries the worker first (lazily constructed, never at module scope), falls back to a new `serializeV3Chunked` in `recordingSchema.ts` (chunks the frame-mapping loop, yields via `setTimeout(0)` every 500 frames, reuses the same private per-frame mappers) when `typeof Worker === 'undefined'`, construction throws, or the worker errors/fails to load.
